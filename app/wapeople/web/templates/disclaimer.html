Accuracy and completeness

We work hard to be accurate, and the site is compiled automatically from official public records. Even so, it may contain errors, omissions, or delays, and the official record always prevails. In particular:

  • Timing: data is refreshed once a day, so very recent actions may not appear yet.
  • Bill stages ("in committee," "passed House," "did not pass," and so on) are our plain-language reading of the Legislature's status codes and votes.
  • Bill summaries are written by the Legislature's nonpartisan staff and describe the version of the bill named with each summary, which may differ from the final law.
  • Older records: step-by-step bill history isn't available from the Legislature for sessions before 2001, and a small number of older vote records are incomplete in the original data.
  • Supreme Court cases are selected by published, rule-based criteria and may not include every relevant decision. Inclusion doesn't mean a case is important or that it changed a law.
  • District boundaries on the map are drawn within about 5 meters of the official lines, for reference only. Don't use them to determine your voting district or for any legal purpose. Check with your county elections office or the Secretary of State.
  • Current legislators are determined from official rosters and voting records. Recent appointments or resignations may take time to appear.

Always confirm important information with the official source linked on each page. If you spot a mistake, please tell us so we can fix it.
